Step-by-Step Meta Setup Walkthrough

1
Create Free Meta Developer Account

Navigate to developers.facebook.com and click "Log In" with your normal Facebook account. Click "Get Started" to verify your free developer account.

2
Create a Meta App (Type: Business)

In the developer dashboard, click "Create App". Select "Other" or "Business" as the use case. Enter any App Name (e.g. "My Social Automation") and click Create.

3
Add "Instagram Graph API" and "Messenger" Products

In the App Dashboard left sidebar, click "Add Product". Add "Instagram Graph API" (for Reels & post comments) and "Messenger" (for private DMs and conversations).

4
Configure Webhooks (Subscribe to comments & messages)

Under Webhooks in your Meta App, select Instagram. Paste the Callback URL and Verify Token from above. Click "Verify and Save". Then click "Subscribe" to the comments and messages fields.

5
Local Testing Mode (Zero-Setup Instant Simulator)

You don't even have to wait for Meta approval! You can immediately click 🧪 Live Simulator in the left dock to test all your triggers, comment auto-replies, and private DM deliveries inside the built-in interactive phone playground.
